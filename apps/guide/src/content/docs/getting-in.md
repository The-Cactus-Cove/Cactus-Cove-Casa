---
title: Getting in
description: Doors, codes and the garage
---

Your stay page controls every door. Keep it open on your phone, or save it to your home screen.

## Front door

Tap **Unlock** on your stay page, or enter your 4 digits on the keypad. The door locks itself shortly after it closes.

## Garage

Open the garage from your stay page. From inside, press the button on the wall. To enter the house, use the door between the garage and the house.

## Having a crowd over?

Use **Keep unlocked for a while** on a door. It locks itself when the time is up.

:::tip[Good to know]
Your 4 digits are the last 4 of the phone number on your booking.
:::
