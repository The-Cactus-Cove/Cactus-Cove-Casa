title: Getting in
description: Doors, codes and the garage
---

Your stay page controls every door.
Keep it open on your phone, or save it to your home screen.

## Front door

Tap **Unlock** on your stay page, or enter your 4 digits on the keypad.
The door locks itself shortly after it closes.

## Garage

Open the garage from your stay page.
Opening and closing the garage door will sound an alarm before the door starts to open.
The garage door can alse be controlled from inside by pressing the button on the wall.

To enter the house, use the door between the garage and the house.
The garage access door uses the same code as the front door.

## Having a crowd over?

From your stay page, use **Keep unlocked for a while** on a door.
It will automatically lock itself when the time is up.

:::tip[Good to know]
Your 4 digits are the last 4 of the phone number on your booking.
:::
