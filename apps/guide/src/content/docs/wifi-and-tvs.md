## TVs

There are five TVs: living room, family room, master bedroom, secondary bedroom and kid's room. Every TV is a Roku. Sign in to your own apps from the Roku home screen, and remember to sign out before you leave.

## Remote, sleep timer, bedtime lockout

From your stay page you can use each TV's remote, set a sleep timer, or lock a TV out overnight, which is handy for the kids' room. A lockout can cover a single night or your whole stay.

:::tip[Good to know]
If a TV doesn't respond to the page, switch it on with its own remote first.
:::
