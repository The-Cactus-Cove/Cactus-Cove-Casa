Fast Wi-Fi covers the whole house, patio included.
Average internet speed is 600 Mbps down / 40 up with 55 ms ping.

You can view the internet speedtest metrics [here](https://speedtest.cactuscove.casa).

## Wi-Fi

The network name and password are on the **Home** tab of your stay page from check-in.
The password is unique to your stay and will be provided at check-in.

## TVs

There are four TVs: living room, family room, master bedroom, and kid's room.
Every TV is a Roku.
Sign in to your own apps from the Roku home screen.
TVs will be reset when you check-out, so you will automatically be signed out.

## Remote, sleep timer, bedtime lockout

From your stay page you can use each TV's remote, set a sleep timer, or lock a TV out overnight, which is handy for the kids' room.
A lockout can cover a single night or your whole stay.
When a lockout is enabled, power to the TV is turned off at the switch so the TV can't be powered on.

:::tip[Good to know]
If a TV doesn't respond to the page, switch it on with its own remote first.
:::
